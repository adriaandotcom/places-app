Privacy, without homework

Your history belongs
in your hands.

No privacy dashboard maze. Here’s how the foundation works.

01

It stays with you.

Observations, places, and search live on your iPhone. No account, tracking server, or automatic cloud backup. You can export or delete your history yourself.

02

You choose the connections.

Apple Maps is off until you enable it. Turning it on makes map requests to Apple. Your timeline and saved places work without it. No third-party services or hosted AI.

03

No guessing dressed as fact.

Missing evidence stays visibly unknown. You can correct a place or journey, and your edits are remembered. The tracker aims to do less when you stay put.

A

A small, independent project.

Built by Adriaan, founder of Simple Analytics. Places is a separate personal project, built in public. The foundation is still in development; real-device tracking and battery testing are part of the work ahead.
